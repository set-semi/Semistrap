Most Semistrap issues fall into a handful of categories. This page covers the fixes for the ones users report most often.

## Windows SmartScreen warning

Windows SmartScreen may show a warning the first time you run Semistrap because it is an unknown program to Microsoft's reputation system, not because it is actually malicious.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click 'More info'">
    On the SmartScreen dialog, click the small **More info** link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Run anyway'">
    Windows reveals a **Run anyway** button. Click it to continue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Installation issues

If the installer fails or exits early:

- Confirm the [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0) is installed.
- Right-click the installer and choose **Run as administrator**.
- Temporarily disable third-party antivirus software during install.
- Redownload from the [latest release page](https://github.com/set-semi/Semistrap/releases/latest) in case the file is corrupted.

## Discord Rich Presence not working

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make sure Discord is running">
    Discord must be open on the same PC before you launch Roblox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Rich Presence is on">
    In Semistrap, open **Settings > Integrations** and verify **Discord Rich Presence** is enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart the game">
    Close Roblox, then relaunch through Semistrap.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If it still does not appear, check Discord's **Settings > Activity Privacy** and confirm "Display current activity as a status message" is enabled.

## Roblox not launching

Try these in order:

1. Restart your PC and launch Roblox again through Semistrap.
2. In Semistrap, reset **Bootstrapper** settings to defaults.
3. Reinstall Semistrap from the [latest release](https://github.com/set-semi/Semistrap/releases/latest).
4. Check the Semistrap logs (accessible from the menu) for specific errors, and include them if you open an issue.

## Still stuck?

Open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/set-semi/Semistrap/issues) with your Windows version, Semistrap version, and any log output. You can also ask in the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/s3QW8b6PUf).
