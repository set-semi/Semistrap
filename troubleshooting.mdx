Most Semistrap issues fall into a small set of categories: SmartScreen prompts, missing prerequisites, RPC not connecting, or a broken Roblox install. Work through the sections below in order and, if nothing helps, export your data and file an issue.

## Windows SmartScreen blocks Semistrap

Windows may show _"Windows protected your PC"_ when you run Semistrap. This happens because Semistrap is not signed by a widely-recognized commercial publisher; it does not mean Semistrap is malicious.

Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## "This app requires .NET" or the installer refuses to run

Semistrap requires the [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0). Install the runtime, then run the Semistrap installer again.

## Discord Rich Presence not working

If your Discord profile does not show the game you are playing:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Restart Discord first, then Roblox">
    Discord's local RPC socket must be running before Semistrap connects to it. Close Roblox, restart Discord, then launch Roblox again from Semistrap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check activity tracking">
    Open **Settings > Integrations** and confirm **Enable activity tracking** is on. Rich Presence is driven by activity tracking; there is no separate RPC toggle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Discord activity privacy">
    In Discord, open **Settings > Activity Privacy** and enable **Share your detected activities with others**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try without Discord's built-in game detection">
    Discord's own game detection can conflict with Semistrap. In Discord **Settings > Registered Games**, remove Roblox if it is listed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Roblox will not launch or crashes on launch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Disable FastFlag overrides">
    Open **Settings > FastFlags** and turn off **Use FastFlag manager**. Bad flags are the most common cause of launch failures.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Force a Roblox reinstall">
    Open **Settings > Bootstrapper** and enable **Force Roblox reinstallation**. Launch Roblox once, then turn it off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check mods">
    If you have custom mods, remove them from the mods folder and try again. A malformed asset can prevent Roblox from starting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Custom bootstrapper looks broken

If the bootstrapper dialog does not render correctly, switch the dialog style in **Settings > Appearance** back to `Semistrap` (the Fluent dialog) and preview it. If it works there, the issue is with your custom theme's XML or referenced assets.

## Getting more help

If none of the above helps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export a support bundle">
    In **Settings > Semistrap**, click **Export data** with both **Config** and **Logs** enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="File an issue">
    Open [a GitHub issue](https://github.com/set-semi/Semistrap/issues) and attach the exported zip. Describe what you did, what happened, and what you expected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or ask on Discord">
    Join the [Semistrap Discord](https://discord.gg/s3QW8b6PUf) and post in the support channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>
