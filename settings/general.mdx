The **General** tab in Semistrap covers baseline application preferences that do not fit under a more specific category. Changes take effect immediately.

## Options

- **Language**: choose the display language for the Semistrap UI.
- **Theme**: light, dark, or match the Windows system theme.
- **Startup behavior**: choose whether Semistrap opens its menu, launches Roblox directly, or stays quiet on launch.
- **Update channel**: pick between stable and pre-release builds.

## Change a general setting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    From the Semistrap menu, click **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select General">
    Choose the **General** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Adjust the options you want. Most settings apply immediately; a few (like update channel) apply on the next launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>
