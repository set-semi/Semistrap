The **Bootstrapper** tab governs how Semistrap starts and manages the Roblox client. This is where advanced launch behavior lives.

## Options

- **Roblox channel**: pick between the standard channel and any supported alternatives.
- **Launch flags**: pass FastFlags or other supported options to Roblox at launch.
- **Graphics overrides**: force specific rendering settings (see [Graphics Config](/features/graphics)).
- **Auto-relaunch**: reopen Roblox automatically if it crashes during a session.
- **Cleanup**: control how Semistrap handles Roblox's cache and installation files between updates.

## Reset to defaults

If a change causes problems, open **Settings > Bootstrapper** and click **Reset to defaults**. Semistrap restores all bootstrapper options to their initial values.

<Warning>
  Only change launch flags you understand. Unknown or unsupported flags can prevent Roblox from starting.
</Warning>
