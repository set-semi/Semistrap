The **Appearance** tab controls how Semistrap itself looks. It does not affect Roblox in-game visuals (see [Graphics Config](/features/graphics) for that).

## Options

- **Theme**: light, dark, or system.
- **Accent color**: pick an accent that matches your Windows setup.
- **Icon set**: choose the icon style used in the Semistrap menu.
- **Compact mode**: shrink padding for smaller displays.

## Change appearance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Appearance">
    Go to **Settings > Appearance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and save">
    Changes preview live in the menu. Close Settings to keep them.
  </Step>
</Steps>
